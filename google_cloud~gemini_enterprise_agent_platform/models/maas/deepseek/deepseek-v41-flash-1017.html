DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017

DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017 is a minor update to DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash from DeepSeek. It uses the same multimodal Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) architecture, accepts text and image input, and has a context window of up to 1M tokens. DeepSeek reports improved results on reasoning, coding, agentic, and visual benchmarks compared to DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash, along with shorter outputs and faster task completion.

The deepseek-v4.1-flash-1017-maas endpoint is available until at least December 31, 2026. Availability might be extended based on usage and demand. For more information, see Minimum availability for Preview models.
